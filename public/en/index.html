How it works

From export to overview in three steps.

  1. 1

    Export

    The data from your pump and sensor come together in Glooko. That is where the export comes from, as a file to download.

  2. 2

    Upload and add

    Glycodex reads the export and organises the readings. You can add your own entries such as mood, notes, activity and blood pressure.

  3. 3

    Understand

    Overview, diary, patterns and Codex show what is in your data. For your next appointment there is the report as a PDF.

Why an export? Glooko offers no public interface for private individuals. That is why Glycodex works with the manual export and shows no real-time data.

Frequently asked questions

Answered briefly.

Your question is missing? Write to me.

Can I use Glycodex?

Not yet. Glycodex is a private project that only I use. If I passed it on to others, it would very likely be a medical device. That requires a company with quality management and regulatory approval. That is exactly why I am looking for partnerships.

Does Glycodex give insulin dosing recommendations?

No. Glycodex describes data and gives no therapy or dosing recommendations. The app only phrases questions about settings as questions for your diabetes care team.

Which devices does Glycodex work with?

Glycodex has been tested with YpsoPump, CamAPS FX and Dexcom G7. The data comes from the Glooko export. I have not tested other devices so far.

Where is the data stored?

Encrypted on my own server. There is a sign-in, roles and read-only access. I decide who gets access. There are no ads.

What is Dextro?

Dextro is the AI companion in Glycodex. It knows the data and answers questions in plain language, without dosing or therapy recommendations. Dextro uses a language model from Anthropic. To answer, the data needed for that answer is sent to this service.

Who is behind Glycodex?

Me, Lina Schröder, as a private individual. Glycodex is independent and has no connection to the companies mentioned.

How can I help?

Tell me what you are missing in your data, and share Glycodex with people who might be interested. If you work in medical technology, I would be glad to have a first conversation.
