For diabetes care teams

Transparent calculations. Calm presentation.

Glycodex is a private diabetes diary that I, a patient with type 1 diabetes, designed and built myself. Here you can see what the app shows, how it calculates and what it deliberately does not do.

About the methodology

Important: Glycodex describes data. The app gives no therapy or dosing recommendations and is not a medical device. It only phrases questions about settings as questions for the diabetes care team.

What Glycodex shows

Four areas, one data source.

It is based on the Glooko exports with the data from YpsoPump, CamAPS FX and Dexcom G7, plus personal entries such as mood, notes, activity and blood pressure.

01

Overview

Time in ranges, based on consensus or a personal target range, and glucose profile (AGP).

02

Diary

Daily curve with glucose, bolus, basal, carbohydrates, activity and personal entries.

03

Patterns · Clinical

Key metrics, events, hypo analysis, nights and daily profiles.

04

Patterns · Technical

Auto mode analysis. The events are derived from basal and glucose and do not appear as such in the export.

Methodology

How Glycodex calculates.

I checked the key metrics against an independent reference calculation.

Basis
International consensus on time in range (Battelino et al., Diabetes Care 2019) and consensus on CGM metrics (Battelino et al., Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2023)
Data requirements
At least 14 days and 70 % sensor time. Otherwise the app points this out.
Events
Start and end after 15 minutes. Extended hypoglycaemia over 120 minutes. Extended hyperglycaemia at 90 out of 120 minutes above 13.9 mmol/L.
Derived values
GMI according to Bergenstal et al. 2018, GRI according to Klonoff et al. 2023
Data specifics
Readings above the measuring range count as “very high”. If a value is recorded twice, the CamAPS value applies.
Target range
Consensus (3.9 to 10.0 mmol/L) or a freely adjustable personal target range. In the report, the consensus remains the default.

For the appointment

Look instead of paperwork.

The report as a PDF

For appointments, Glycodex creates a report that sums up the most important pages at a glance.

  • Key metrics based on consensus
  • Glucose profile and times of day
  • Questions for the diabetes care team
  • Daily profiles
  • Auto mode analysis

Read-only access

Your own login with a password. View only, no changes possible.

One-time link

For a single appointment. The link works exactly once and then gives access for a few hours.

The data is stored encrypted on my own server. I decide who has access.

Limits

What Glycodex is not.

  • Not a medical device and no substitute for the assessment by the diabetes care team
  • Depends on the manual Glooko export, no real-time data
  • The auto mode analysis is a derived observation, not information from the manufacturer
