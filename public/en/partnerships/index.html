For medical technology companies

Diabetes data from a patient's perspective. With a working prototype.

Glycodex reads the exports from Glooko and turns pump and sensor data into a calm, clear overview. I use the app every day. So that it can help many people, I am looking for partnerships.

What I am looking for

Starting point

288 readings a day. Shown, but hardly explained.

Lots of data, little understanding

Existing portals show the numbers but hardly explain them from the perspective of the people who live with them.

The closed loop stays invisible

When and how the algorithm steps in is hard to follow in everyday life.

Wellbeing and data kept apart

In my research I found no app that connects mood, notes and everyday life with the measured values in this way.

Data path

With CamAPS, the data runs through Glooko. There is no public interface for private individuals there, only the manual export.

Status today

Used daily

A prototype in real everyday life.

Technology
Web app on a private server, encrypted storage, roles, read-only access and one-time links
Data

How Glycodex came about

Self-directed, step by step.

Built with Claude by Anthropic. The idea, requirements, design, testing and sign-off of every version were my responsibility.

  1. 1

    Research before building

    Pain points of people with diabetes, existing apps, medical device law, the EU AI Act and data protection

  2. 2

    Setting principles

    Privacy first, describe instead of judge, no dosing recommendations, calm design

  3. 3

    Developing the brand

    Name, logo and a brand system with colours, typography and voice

  4. 4

    Building in short cycles

    Notes from everyday life, suggestions and questions, implementation, testing, approval

  5. 5

    Running it securely

    Private server and domain, sign-in, roles, one-time links and encryption

  6. 6

    Fine-tuning

    Three design rounds. Before and after every change, all displayed figures were compared.
