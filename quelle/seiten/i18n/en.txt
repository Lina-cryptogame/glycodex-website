123	Can I use Glycodex?⟦0⟧
124	Not yet. Glycodex is a private project that only I use. If I passed it on to others, it would very likely be a medical device. That requires a company with quality management and regulatory approval. That is exactly why I am looking for partnerships.
125	Does Glycodex give insulin dosing recommendations?⟦0⟧
126	No. Glycodex describes data and gives no therapy or dosing recommendations. The app only phrases questions about settings as questions for your diabetes care team.
127	Which devices does Glycodex work with?⟦0⟧
128	Glycodex has been tested with YpsoPump, CamAPS FX and Dexcom G7. The data comes from the Glooko export. I have not tested other devices so far.
129	Where is the data stored?⟦0⟧
130	Encrypted on my own server. There is a sign-in, roles and read-only access. I decide who gets access. There are no ads.
131	What is Dextro?⟦0⟧
132	Dextro is the AI companion in Glycodex. It knows the data and answers questions in plain language, without dosing or therapy recommendations. Dextro uses a language model from Anthropic. To answer, the data needed for that answer is sent to this service.

175	About the methodology
176	<b style="font-weight: 500">Important:</b> Glycodex describes data. The app gives no therapy or dosing recommendations and is not a medical device. It only phrases questions about settings as questions for the diabetes care team.
177	What Glycodex shows
178	Four areas, one data source.
179	It is based on the Glooko exports with the data from YpsoPump, CamAPS FX and Dexcom G7, plus personal entries such as mood, notes, activity and blood pressure.
180	Overview
181	Time in ranges, based on consensus or a personal target range, and glucose profile (AGP).
182	Diary

184	Patterns · Clinical

187	Patterns · Technical
188	Auto mode analysis. The events are derived from basal and glucose and do not appear as such in the export.
189	Methodology
190	How Glycodex calculates.
191	I checked the key metrics against an independent reference calculation.
192	Basis
193	International consensus on time in range (Battelino et al., Diabetes Care 2019) and consensus on CGM metrics (Battelino et al., Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol 2023)
194	Data requirements
195	At least 14 days and 70 % sensor time. Otherwise the app points this out.
196	Events
197	Start and end after 15 minutes. Extended hypoglycaemia over 120 minutes. Extended hyperglycaemia at 90 out of 120 minutes above 13.9 mmol/L.
198	Derived values
199	GMI according to Bergenstal et al. 2018, GRI according to Klonoff et al. 2023
200	Data specifics
201	Readings above the measuring range count as “very high”. If a value is recorded twice, the CamAPS value applies.
202	Target range
203	Consensus (3.9 to 10.0 mmol/L) or a freely adjustable personal target range. In the report, the consensus remains the default.
